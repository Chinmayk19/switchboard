# Implementation decisions

Switchboard delivers workspace-scoped OIDC login and logout, the React inbox, durable message delivery, real-time replay, encrypted private notes, and optional Paybox subscriptions.

- **Persistence and recovery.** MongoDB holds sessions, inbox state, pending work, and replay history. Journaled writes precede acknowledgements. Unique indexes deduplicate messages and make claims atomic. Shared leases coordinate provider requests, and stable idempotency keys survive process restarts. The event projector persists reservations so another instance can finish them.
- **Access and encryption.** The IdP is the source of workspace membership and roles. The server scopes every protected read and write to those claims. Browsers encrypt notes with workspace keys shared outside Switchboard. This keeps keys out of the database and makes recovery and rotation the team's responsibility.
- **Billing.** Trial, incomplete, and past-due workspaces can send until Paybox reports cancellation. Canceled workspaces can still read messages. Previously accepted replies wait for sending to resume. Purchased seat counts do not change IdP membership. The UI explains proration and credits, links to Paybox for bank approval, and shows reconciled state.

**Scope and limitations.** Elasticsearch search and member provisioning were skipped. WebSocket delivery polls MongoDB and replay data has no retention bound. Long simulator outages can outlast webhook retries, and exact-once sends depend on Pigeon retaining its keys. Concurrent grader reset during live traffic and hard-kill recovery during a payment mutation remain unverified. Billing state can lag during provider outages. Notes have no server-side recovery or retroactive key revocation. The saved public check scored 75/75. The private level 4 checker and an official bonus checker were unavailable.
